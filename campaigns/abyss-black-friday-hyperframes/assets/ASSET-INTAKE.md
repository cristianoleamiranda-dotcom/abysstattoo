# ABYSS BLACK FRIDAY — Asset intake

This campaign is an editable 30-second, 9:16 HyperFrames project. This folder is a production intake checklist; it does not contain finished campaign media yet.

## Upload these original assets

Please upload the files to Dropbox (or attach them in this chat). Keep originals at the highest available quality.

| Asset | Recommended source | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| ABYSS logo | SVG or transparent PNG; monochrome version if available | Hook, end card |
| Studio | 3–6 vertical or horizontal clips/photos of the real studio | Scene 02 |
| Tattoos | 3–6 close-ups of real healed/in-progress work, with client permission | Scene 03 |
| Piercing and jewelry | 3–6 close-ups of actual piercings and jewelry | Scene 04 |
| Tattoo machines | 2–4 photos or clips of the actual equipment | Scene 05 |
| Gift card | Existing artwork, or a photo of the real printed/digital card | Scene 06 |
| Audio | Licensed/original music or a track you have permission to use | Full edit |

## Mobile upload from Android

1. Open Dropbox and create a folder named `ABYSS_BLACK_FRIDAY_ASSETS`.
2. Upload the original logo, studio footage, tattoo footage, piercing/jewelry, machine images, gift card and audio.
3. Paste the Dropbox folder link into this chat.

You can also attach the assets directly in this conversation. If you use Dropbox, set the link permission so that the files can be viewed by the connected Dropbox account.

## Production rules

- Preserve the real ABYSS identity and the appearance of actual people, tattoos, jewelry and equipment.
- Do not invent discounts, prices, dates, stock, booking conditions or promotional terms.
- Use AI only for atmosphere, transitions, abstract backgrounds and motion support; do not fabricate products or studio evidence.
- Confirm that any identifiable client footage has permission for advertising use.
- Keep the approved direction: HYBRID CINEMATIC — luxury editorial meets dark industrial; obsidian black, graphite, bone white and restrained chrome.
- Endline: **ART. DISCIPLINE. LEGACY.**

## Current state

The seven-scene HyperFrames structure exists in `index.html` and `compositions/`. This project is a storyboard/editable motion skeleton, not a rendered MP4. Do not mark the campaign as finished until the real assets are placed, the HyperFrames check passes, and the final render is reviewed.
