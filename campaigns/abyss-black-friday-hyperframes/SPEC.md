# Creative specification — ABYSS BLACK FRIDAY

## Goal
Make viewers associate ABYSS TATTOO STUDIO with considered craft, precision and a premium tattoo / piercing experience, then direct them to the studio's approved booking channel.

## Output
- HyperFrames composition
- 1080 × 1920 (9:16)
- 30 fps
- 30 seconds / 900 frames
- Designed for Reels and Stories; recompose separately for 4:5 and 16:9

## Look
- Ground: graphite / obsidian, subtle material variation, no empty black void
- Ink: bone white (#F1EEE7)
- Accent: restrained brushed chrome (#AEB3B8), used only for precision / product details
- Display: Archivo Black / League Gothic, uppercase, large, compact tracking
- Supporting type: Space Mono, small editorial metadata
- Surfaces: brushed metal, matte black, satin card stock; use real source footage for the studio, skin, jewelry, machines and gift card
- Contrast: strong but retain detail in shadows; no crushed blacks

## Motion
- Hero title arrivals: 0.3–0.6 s, long-tail ease-out
- Camera-like moves: 0.4–0.6 s, power2.inOut
- Letter stagger: 0.06–0.10 s
- Cuts: every 2–4 s; each transition caused by a visible object / direction
- No continuous drift after titles land
- Final brand card holds at least 2 s

## Banned
- Generic sale badges, fake price tags, invented offers or dates
- Neon, purple / blue gradients, excessive glitch, floating particles
- AI-invented tattoos, studio interiors, people, jewelry or machines presented as real
- Distorted anatomy, hands, skin, piercing placements or product geometry
- Logo redrawn from memory
- Small text outside social safe zones
- Unlicensed music or stock footage without rights

## QA
- First frame is a readable hook, not a black screen
- All essential copy inside the 9:16 safe area
- No text under platform UI rails
- Every media slot replaced with verified ABYSS source material before final delivery
- Logo and CTA match supplied official assets
- HyperFrames lint clean; inspect transition frames and final end-card hold
